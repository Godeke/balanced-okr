# Balanced OKRs

Sales hit its acquisition target. Should the campaign expand before those customers renew?

An Objective describes the change a team wants. A Key Result measures progress. A Guardrail sets a boundary or review trigger for an adverse consequence. The team agrees who will act if a boundary is crossed or evidence arrives after the quarter ends.

- [Read the book](book/README.md) to follow the acquisition decision through first renewal and explore incentives, shared capacity, and evidence.
