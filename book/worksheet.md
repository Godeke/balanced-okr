# Balanced OKR worksheet

Use one copy per Objective. Fill in **Before the work** with the people who own the decision and the work. Return to the same copy when results arrive. Add another Key Result only when it would change a decision. If you add a Guardrail, copy its response row with it. [Open the Markdown source to copy](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Godeke/balanced-okr/main/book/worksheet.md)

## Before the work

| Field | Replace the bracketed prompts |
| --- | --- |
| Objective | We want [change] for [customers, team, or business]. |
| Owner and period | [Name or role] owns the outcome for [period]. |
| Key Result | For [defined population], move [measure] from [baseline and baseline period] to [target] by [date]. Use [data source and counting rule]. |
| Guardrail | Watch for [material harm to whom]. Review or act if [event or threshold] occurs among [population] over [observation window]. Compare with [reference, if relevant]. |
| Response to the Guardrail | [Reviewer] checks [evidence] by [date]. If the boundary is crossed, [decision owner] will [first action]. If a breakdown would clarify the finding, examine [specific evidence]. |
| While evidence is pending | Until [evidence arrives], continue [agreed scope]. Only [decision owner] may authorize [specific change in scope] early. Record the reason and revisit date below. |

**If another team participates**, ask them to complete this sentence. We can commit [capacity or deliverable] by [date]. That means [work or date affected]. Our contact is [name].

**If the approach is untested**, complete this sentence together. We think [action] will improve [outcome] for [group]. We will compare [alternatives], account for [cost], and use [evidence] to decide whether to expand, change, stop, or test again.

## At review

Copy a row for each decision. Keep earlier entries so the team that sees the mature outcome can recover what was known at the time.

| Review date | KR actual vs. target | Guardrail finding and evidence | Decision, reason, and follow-up |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Quarter close] | [Actual, target, period] | [Within boundary, breached, or pending. What supports that finding?] | [Owner, action, reason, next review date] |
| [When delayed evidence matures, if applicable] | [Refer to the earlier result or record a correction] | [Observed result, comparison, what remains uncertain] | [Owner, action, reason, next review date] |

When evidence is pending, record the KR result and keep the overall judgment provisional. A material breach calls for its own decision. A stronger KR cannot offset it. A decision to keep working under an agreed business tolerance leaves the breach on the record. Legal and safety obligations remain binding.

**At the next planning review**, decide whether to keep, change, or retire the measure. What behavior did it encourage? What decision did it change?
